Questions

Frequently asked

Is the report really free?

Yes. Your custom Boat Demand Report is completely free with no obligation. You'll see your market estimate, recommended plan, and budget, and you can download the proposal as a PDF.

How does weather-triggered advertising work?

We identify the weather moments that drive boating demand in your market — warm weekends, sunny holidays, first frost, freeze warnings — and activate your ads to in-market buyers exactly when those conditions hit, pausing when they don't.

Who will actually see my ads?

Households in your market that are actively shopping for boats, layered with marina, ramp & state-park geotargeting and location look-back (people recently seen at the water), across Connected TV, streaming radio, podcasts, display, and digital out-of-home.

Where do the numbers in my report come from?

Population, household, boat-owner, and location figures are planning estimates built from your geography, market density, and standard industry assumptions — not audited counts. They are there to size the opportunity and set a budget, and a Smart 1 strategist verifies the locations and figures before anything goes live.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

SmartForecast Ads is a month-to-month plan. Unused budget from slow-weather windows rolls forward as credit, so your media keeps working even when the weather doesn't.

How fast can we launch?

Most dealers can launch within days. Start with the free report, and a Smart 1 strategist can review it and build your campaign from there.
