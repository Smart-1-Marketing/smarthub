1. Open Client 360 for Monogram Homes.
2. In Proposals, press **Execute** beside the uploaded proposal.
3. Confirm the Proposal Execution page preselects Monogram Homes and the proposal.
4. Press **Analyze Proposal**.
5. Confirm branches exist for Retargeting, Paid Search, SEO + AI, Stadium to Screen, Meta, YouTube Ads, Social, YouTube Sales Video, YouTube Channel Optimization and AI Advertising.
