Letting clients reach their own approvals here

A client opening {{ origin }}/suite-app has no Hub account and is never given one. Smart 1 Suite says which sub‑account they are in, the Hub resolves that to the client it belongs to, and hands them their own content — the same approval and proposal links they are already emailed. It shows them nothing new.

It is not part of the setup above, and it needs one thing first: each client’s sub‑account has to be recorded against them in the Hub. Without that a client opening it is told we cannot tell whose account they are in, which is correct and reads to them as broken.

Add this link only once those are recorded. Getting the sub‑account wrong shows one client another client’s record, and nothing on screen would say so.

If a page refuses to load

Only a short allowlist of read‑only pages can be framed. That is deliberate — the Hub has destructive buttons behind its login, and a page that would load, look complete and fail on save is worse than one that says so.
