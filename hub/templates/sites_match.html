Match sites to clients

A Simvoly project links to a client through internal_client_name, filled in by hand — so with a thousand projects it's mostly empty, and the margin report has no revenue to compare cost against. This matches on domain, which identifies a company exactly where a name doesn't: "Riverside HVAC" and "Riverside HVAC LLC" are two strings for one business, but the domain is one value.

Where there is no domain to match on it falls back to the name, and reads it the way Simvoly projects are actually named: "TMRG - JWS Pottery" is a media partner, a business, and nothing that matches a client until the partner comes off the front. A trailing "2026 Refresh" or "Landing Page" comes off the end the same way. A project called Anna's Website or S1M Test names no business at all and is said to name nobody rather than matched loosely — and a name two clients answer to proposes neither. Every name match is a suggestion you tick; only a domain match is ticked for you.

Orphan URLs — a website with no client on it

Every URL the Hub holds that nobody is attached to: a Knack website record with no organization, a live Simvoly project with no internal client name, a site scan and a Google access request nobody ever filed against a client. Search it, pick the client, attach — and the attachment is written to the client registry, their Client 360 record, the Simvoly project and the Knack website record, each reported separately. A client can have several: the shop, the landing pages, the microsite. Attaching one does not replace the last.

Clients with no website on file

A client with no URL can't be joined to a scan, a brand lookup, a Simvoly project or anything else keyed on domain — they are invisible to every cross-tool report. Their website is rarely actually missing, though: it is in a different table. This reads the click-thru on their live products, the Knack website registry, our own live Simvoly projects, their site scans and their Google access requests, and proposes the domain those agree on. Two independent sources agreeing is close to proof; one is a suggestion. Nothing is written until you accept it.
