Match sites to clients

A Simvoly project links to a client through internal_client_name, filled in by hand — so with a thousand projects it's mostly empty, and the margin report has no revenue to compare cost against. This matches on domain, which identifies a company exactly where a name doesn't: "Riverside HVAC" and "Riverside HVAC LLC" are two strings for one business, but the domain is one value.

Clients with no website on file

A client with no URL can't be joined to a scan, a brand lookup, a Simvoly project or anything else keyed on domain — they are invisible to every cross-tool report. Their website is rarely actually missing, though: it is in a different table. This reads the click-thru on their live products, the Knack website registry, our own live Simvoly projects, their site scans and their Google access requests, and proposes the domain those agree on. Two independent sources agreeing is close to proof; one is a suggestion. Nothing is written until you accept it.
