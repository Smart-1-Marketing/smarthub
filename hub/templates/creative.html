Creative

Everything for making things for clients — images, video, audio and ad copy.

🎨

Image Creator

A full graphics editor built on Fabric.js. Stock photos from Pexels, Pixabay & Unsplash in one search, Brandfetch logos & brand colours, Iconify icons, Google Fonts, AI generation, and your own client gallery & audit imagery. Projects save as editable canvases you can reopen.
🖼

Client Image Uploads

Build a client's image library by industry. One search across Pexels, Pixabay and Unsplash, sized for the web on the way in, filed against the client and pushable to their GoHighLevel media.
📈

Page Image Optimizer

Scan a live client page for oversized images, then fix the worst ones five at a time. Comes back with replacement <img> tags and the page weight you saved.
🖼

Image Optimizer & Resizer

Crop, resize and compress a single image toward an exact pixel size and KB budget. For naming a batch for SEO, use the SEO Image Pipeline instead.
✂

Background Remover

Transparent PNG cut-outs via remove.bg. Resize before the cut to control credits and after it to control the file, then save straight to the client gallery against a client.
🏷

SEO Image Pipeline

Batch up to 5 images and let AI write the SEO filename & alt text for that client’s page. Review and edit every one, then save as optimised WebP to the client gallery — with a searchable archive kept per client and project.
🎬

Commercial Builder

Full :05–:60 CTV and YouTube spots — concept, timed script, storyboard, voice, QR end card and render. Eleven automatic QC checks gate the render rather than advising on it.
🎧

Fan Radio

Football-themed :15 and :30 spots across three dayparts, voiced through ElevenLabs, on a share page the client can listen to and approve.
🔊

Radio Promo

Scripted radio spots with AI copy and ElevenLabs voicing, a voice library and a saved-spot archive.
📰

Landing Page Ads

Reads a Smart 1 Suite landing page and generates matching responsive search, social, display and audio ads.
Where the output goes

Approved images land in the client's gallery, commercials and radio spots save to their library, and ad copy attaches to the campaign it was written for. Anything produced here appears on that client's 360 record under “Work for this client”.
