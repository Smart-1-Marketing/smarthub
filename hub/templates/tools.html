Sales

Proposal Builder

Build a client proposal from the real rate card — goals, target areas, products, three packages — then send it as a branded PDF, file it on the client's record and open the opportunity in Smart 1 Suite. Converts straight to an insertion order. This replaces the separate builder that used to live at /sales/proposals.
💻

Landing Page Maker

Build a campaign landing page from a saved proposal. It reads the client, the products being sold and their brand from Brandfetch, so the page, the ads and the IO all say the same thing. Copy is written from the proposal and their own site — it will leave a section out rather than invent a review, an offer or a guarantee. The finished page posts its leads into the Leads panel.

📝

IO Builder

Build an insertion order and submit it into Smart 1 Suite. Now inside the Hub rather than a separate site, so it can look up a client from the registry and prefill what we already know about them. Its URL stays /tools/io, so every existing link keeps working.
