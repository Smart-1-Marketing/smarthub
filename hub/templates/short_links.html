Client Link Masking

A link a client opens — a proposal, a preview, a review page — masked under our own domain instead of the raw Hub address, with every open counted by Short.io. The slug is client/month/year/project, each part built from what is typed below; the client segment is the same slug every other client join in this Hub already uses, so it stays consistent with the rest of the record. Masking asks Short.io to keep the address bar on our domain rather than redirecting to the real one — it works for anything hosted here, and depends on the destination allowing itself to be framed when it is not.
