Customer Voices

Create a reusable voice from a customer's recordings. Choose it in Radio Ad Creator, Fan Radio, or Commercial Builder whenever you create speech.

Add a customer voice

Use 1–5 clear recordings of a single speaker, ideally 1–3 minutes total. Avoid music, background noise, and other speakers. MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, OGG, or WebM; up to 25 MB per file and 50 MB total. Recordings are sent to ElevenLabs for cloning. Smart 1 Hub keeps the voice details, not a copy of the recordings.

Use a voice already available on the connected ElevenLabs account, including a clone created earlier.
