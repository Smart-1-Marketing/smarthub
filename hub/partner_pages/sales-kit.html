Local Service Ads & Performance Max

Search & Local

Two Google formats built for local service businesses and cross-channel automation.

Local Service Ads (LSAs) are Google Ads designed specifically for local, service-based businesses. They appear at the very top of search results when someone searches for services in their area. LSAs operate on a pay-per-lead model — you only pay when a customer contacts you directly through the ad — and often feature a "Google Guaranteed" badge that adds trust and credibility.

LSAs are a direct line to local customers actively seeking your services. They're particularly effective for plumbers, electricians, locksmiths, and home repair services.

Performance Max (PMax) is Google's most automated campaign type, designed to achieve specific goals across all of Google's channels. You set a goal — sales, leads, website traffic — and Google's AI works to achieve it across Search, Display, YouTube, Gmail and Maps.

Connected TV & Advanced TV (OTT)

Video, Audio & Outdoor

Television advertising with the precision and measurability of digital.

Both Connected TV (CTV) and Over-the-Top (OTT) deliver video content via the internet, but there's a key distinction. CTV means your smart TV or devices like Roku and Amazon Fire Stick — video content watched on these big screens through internet-connected apps.

Advanced TV / OTT is broader. It includes any video streamed over the internet, whether it's on a phone, tablet, computer, gaming console, Blu-ray player or CTV device. The exciting part: these ads are clickable, unlike traditional TV commercials.

What you get
  • Targeted audience: Reach specific demographics and interests with precision.

  • Cross-device reach: Reach your audience on multiple devices, from phones to living room TVs.
