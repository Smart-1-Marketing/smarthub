ATV
Channels & Media
Advanced TV
A marketing technique that combines digital technologies with data to improve targeting, measurement and personalization. ATV lets advertisers serve different ads to different audience segments while showing the same linear TV content, delivering more relevant ad content to each viewer. It combines the reach of linear TV with data-driven precision — demographic profile, viewing behavior, interests and location. In clickable inventory, success is measured by view-to-completion rate plus click or conversion rate.

CTV
Channels & Media
Connected TV
Literally a TV connected to the internet. A television might use a connecting device such as an Xbox or Roku box to become connected, or it may be a newer Smart TV with internet connectivity and OTT apps built in. Because of how consumption happens on a big screen, success is traditionally measured by view-to-completion rate: did the consumer watch all or nearly all of your :15 or :30 commercial?
  • CTV ads have a high degree of native brand safety.
  • CTV ads can be targeted using either third-party or first-party data, and are bought in flexible, discrete units rather than linear TV's requirements.
  • CTV viewers in many cases can't skip or fast-forward through ads, so viewability can be quite high.

IP Targeting
Channels & Media
A digital marketing technique that uses a consumer's IP address to deliver online advertisements. IP stands for Internet Protocol, the set of rules governing the format of all data sent via the internet; an IP address uniquely identifies an internet-connected device. IP targeting lets marketers find an exact audience and put ads directly in their line of sight, reaching any device connected to the WiFi network in question — laptop, desktop, tablet or smartphone. If you have ever run a direct mail campaign, IP targeting lets you use those same addresses to reach those consumers in digital, reinforcing your original message.

OTT

Over-the-Top
A way to provide content to viewers over the internet, bypassing the traditional cable box. OTT content can be viewed on many platforms including smart TVs, computers, tablets, mobile phones and gaming consoles, and can include video streaming, audio streaming and other platforms. OTT is often used interchangeably with ATV, but OTT really describes how the content is delivered. In clickable inventory, success is measured by view-to-completion rate plus click or conversion rate.
