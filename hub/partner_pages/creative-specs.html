Rebuilt for 202620 formats updated · 3 added

Every value was re-checked against the platform’s current documentation. Each card carries a What changed since the 2025 kit panel. The corrections most likely to affect live work:

  • Never require FLV in VAST. Flash support ended in December 2020. Specify VAST 4.x.
  • Meta’s 20% text-overlay rule no longer exists. There is no limit on text in an ad image.
  • “TrueView” is not a format. Google made it a metric in October 2025 — ask for skippable in-stream or in-feed video.
  • YouTube’s file limit is 256 GB, not 10 MB. The old figure was wrong by four orders of magnitude.
  • CTV loudness (–24 LKFS) was missing — it is the single most common cause of creative rejection.
  • X Direct Message Cards and Conversational Ads are gone. Do not sell against them.
  • Gmail clips email over 102 KB, breaking tracking pixels and unsubscribe links.

Desktop Display

UpdatedDisplay
  • Creative must be clearly distinguishable from site content — a contrasting border or non-white background.
  • A 1-pixel contrasting border is a publisher convention, not a formal IAB standard. Build it in by default.
  • Animation: no single universal limit. Google Ads allows 30 seconds and requires animation to stop; DV360 caps at 30 seconds; The Trade Desk allows 15 seconds of looping. IAB now guides on CPU load rather than duration.
  • Availability for non-standard ad units varies — these require a custom quote.

Connected TV / OTT

UpdatedVideo & Audio
  • Loudness is the most common cause of CTV creative rejection. Deliver to spec.
  • 1080p remains the delivery standard. IAB recognizes 4K but no major publisher requires it.
  • Connected TV devices are not clickable by nature. Netflix prohibits QR codes in creative.

What changed since the 2025 kit — 5 updates
  • Loudness spec added — it was missing and it is the top rejection reason.
  • Durations corrected: publishers run :06, :15, :20, :30, :45 and :60 — not just :15–:30.
  • Bitrate split into mezzanine versus ready-to-serve. The old sheet conflated the two.
  • Frame rate broadened — match the source; constant frame rate only.
  • IAB's new CTV formats (Pause, Menu, Screensaver, In-Scene, Squeezeback, Overlay) published December 2025.

HTML file requirements
  • Table-based layout with inline CSS

Dark mode and accessibility
  • Apple Mail fully inverts light-background emails; Gmail partially inverts; Outlook desktop does little or nothing. Give logos a visible outline.
  • Target WCAG 2.x Level AA — aligns with ADA, the European Accessibility Act and Canada's ACA.
  • Minimum 14 px desktop / 16 px mobile body text, 1.5× line height.
  • Use live HTML text rather than text baked into images. Verify contrast ratios.
What changed since the 2025 kit — 4 updates
  • Width corrected — 600 px is the safe default; 750 px is too wide for Classic Outlook, which ignores media queries.
  • Gmail's 102 KB clipping cap added. Exceed it and Gmail truncates the message, breaking tracking pixels and the unsubscribe footer.
  • Dark mode testing added — three different rendering behaviors across clients.
  • Accessibility added — the European Accessibility Act took effect in 2025.
