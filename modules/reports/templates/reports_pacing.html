Budget (period) is the monthly media budget prorated to the part of this month the flight covers. Pace is spent over expected-to-date; under is below 0.90, over is past 1.10. Stalled is two completed days of zero spend mid-flight; unmapped is a sold line with no confirmed campaign filed against it — a campaign the auto-mapper filed from its name paces nothing until somebody confirms it, and the row says how many are waiting. An alert needs the same off-pace band three days running. Projected is spent plus the last seven days' average times the days remaining. Raw media spend, staff only.
