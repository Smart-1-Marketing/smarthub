{# The client picker, shared by the unmapped queue and the budgets form. One implementation rather than one per form: it types ahead over hub/clients_registry through this module's own /reports/api/clients, and hands the form both the display name and the Hub client key, so a mapping is filed under the same key every other module's record uses. Picking a client fires a `s1reports:client` event on the input, which the unmapped queue listens to for the rename hint. #}
