The product is what the client reads: two campaigns on two platforms filed under one product are one bar on their page. The box offers the Smart 1 catalog and opens on the platform's default; anything else may be typed. A mapping with no product shows under a generic label and is flagged on the client's page.

"Looks like" is what the book and the names say: the client the ad account's other campaigns are confirmed as, the client whose name the campaign name resembles — the client's name or domain in it, or a near spelling — and the client the account's own name resembles. The picker opens on the likeliest one so Map is one press. Two clients alike (an account saying one and a name saying another counts), or a likeness under {{ file_pct }}%, is why the auto-mapper left the row here rather than filing it: it is a suggestion for a person, and the client somebody refused is never suggested again.

The rename hint is the name shape the auto-mapper reads: {{ shape }}. A campaign named that way is filed without anybody opening this page, and waits above for a confirmation. Pick a client on a row and the hint fills in that client's key.
