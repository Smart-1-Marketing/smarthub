Build a client's month of organic posts in one pass, then export it for Smart 1 Suite's Social Planner. The plan starts from what the Hub already knows about the client — their services, their brand, their image gallery — so the work is editing rather than authoring. Nothing publishes from here: the export is a CSV you upload under Social Planner → Bulk Upload.

Everything below is a fact the writer may use. A price, a discount, a phone number or a deadline that does not appear here is flagged as a blocking problem before the plan can be approved — not because the model is untrustworthy, but because it has no way to know what the client actually authorised.

Without this the month spreads itself evenly across everything the business does — which is how a plan ends up promoting the service they least want more of. These are fed to the writer as the focus, not forced into every post.
