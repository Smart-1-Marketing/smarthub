Upload marketing expenses

Optional

Most accounting systems can export this in a few clicks. Any of these work:

  • A monthly breakdown of advertising and marketing expenses
  • A running 12 months of advertising expenses

Seeing the actual line items is what lets us spot duplicate services, overlapping vendors, and subscriptions nobody is using — the cost savings that a category-by-category estimate will always miss.
