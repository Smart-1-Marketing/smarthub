4 Cast {{ help_dot('radio_promo.build.cast') if help_dot is defined else '' }}

Clone an authorized voice from recordings

Upload: 1–5 clear recordings of one speaker only, ideally 1–5 minutes total. Use MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, OGG, or WebM; no music, calls, room echo, or other speakers. Keep each file under 25 MB. The files are sent directly to ElevenLabs and are not saved by Smart 1 Hub.

A bed is composed to the length of the spot it sits under, so nothing has to be trimmed to fit. Each tile shows the words it will actually send. Bring your own track instead, or run the spot as a straight read with no bed at all — all three are real answers.
