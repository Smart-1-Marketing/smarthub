How a lead is captured

A visitor types their website. We run our own pre-check — robots.txt, structured data, sitemap, titles, content — in about five seconds, free, and show them a score and the three worst findings.

To see the rest they give a name, business, email and phone. The lead is written to the database, delivery to Suite starts, and only then does a paid Insites audit start — so no anonymous visitor and no bot can ever spend a credit. That handover is what the Leads column counts; everyone who ran the free check and stopped is the smaller number underneath it.

A domain already scanned in the last {{ reuse_days }} days reuses that audit and spends nothing. The visitor only ever sees the SEO & AEO report; the other seven audits stay in the Hub.

Leads land in the leads panel with every other capture, tagged with the placement and carrying a link to the report PDF.
