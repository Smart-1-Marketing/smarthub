Freeform US address. The Census geocoder answers with every match it holds; pick the one that names your dock, not the one that names the road it is on.


2. What kind of place is this?

The choice decides which tiles the page tries to fill; a tile with no feed collapses rather than draws blank.

3. What the probe found

Sources it is sure of land on the page; sources with more than one candidate need you to pick one; sources with no candidate collapse. You can uncheck any row.
